I work by three principles on every project: alignment, agreement, accountability. Know who's doing what, agree on what we're actually building before we start, and own it fully when something goes wrong. I like independence, running a pipeline solo for years taught me that, but most of what I'm proud of came from building something alongside a team, not alone. If it involves figuring out how to make a process actually work, I'm probably going to enjoy it.

Open to new opportunities in marketing, content, and multimedia production. Let's build something great together.
