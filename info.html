Services

Graphics & Branding

Identity work, campaign key art, editorial layout, and the templates that keep a brand consistent once the launch is over. I design the system, not just the single asset, so the next person to touch it has something to follow.

From one poster to a full brand kit.

Video & Short Film

Shooting and editing for campaigns, showcases, and narrative pieces. Comfortable running a shoot solo when the budget is tight, and comfortable directing a small crew when it is not.

Concept, shoot, edit, deliver.

Motion Graphics

Titles, lower thirds, animated logos, and social cutdowns. Motion is where a static identity proves whether it was built properly, so this usually runs alongside the branding rather than after it.

Where the identity starts moving.

Music & Sound

Track beds, sound design, and audio cleanup for video and podcast work. Sound is half of what makes an edit feel finished and it is usually the first thing to get cut from a schedule.

The half of video nobody budgets for.

Podcast & Presentations

Episode production end to end, and decks that hold up in a room rather than just on a screen. Both are really the same problem: making a long argument hold someone's attention.

Long-form, made to hold up.

Content Strategy

Working out the calendar, the formats, and the process behind the output so a channel can keep running without me. This is the part most people skip, and it is the reason their social presence stalls after a month.

The process behind it all.
